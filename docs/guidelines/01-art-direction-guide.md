# Art Direction Guide

## Vision

Professional cinematic exploration experience.

Not cartoon.

Not fantasy game.

Not pirate game.

The portfolio should feel like:

* Premium
* Atmospheric
* Mature
* Professional

---

# Inspirations

Primary

* Sea of Thieves
* Assassin's Creed Black Flag
* Journey

Secondary

* Uncharted
* Ghost of Tsushima
* The Witcher 3 environments

---

# Color Palette

Ocean

* Deep navy
* Midnight blue

Lighting

* Moonlight silver
* Fog white

Accents

* Treasure gold
* Warm lantern amber

---

# Avoid

* Neon colors
* Bright UI colors
* Cartoon assets
* Excessive pirate stereotypes
* Skulls everywhere
* Cheap fantasy themes

---

# Visual Goal

Users should think:

"This feels like a premium interactive experience."

Not:

"This is a pirate website."
